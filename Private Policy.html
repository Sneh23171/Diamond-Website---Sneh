Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Diamond Custom Jewelry ("Diamond," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you access our website (the "Website") and provide information through our forms. Automatically Collected Information : We may also collect certain information automatically when you visit our Website. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, and other technical details.

Security Measures : We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We use industry-standard security measures such as encryption and firewalls to safeguard your data.
